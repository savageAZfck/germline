# Security

Report vulnerabilities privately to savagetism@icloud.com — do not open
public issues for exploitable weaknesses.

Scope: genesis dual-signature verification, generation arithmetic, bequest provenance/revocability checks, lineage tree integrity, descent verification.

Out of scope: the contents of bequest payloads (they are content-hash references, not data), and the parent-child relationship after birth — that is wolakota's scope (see THREAT_MODEL.md).
